See what's due

One list for the whole home, sorted for you.

Today

What's overdue, what's due and what's done, and what's yours next. Tap the circle to tick one off, with Undo if you tap the wrong one.

Any schedule

Every few days, or on set days, like trash night on Tuesdays and Saturdays. Doing one late doesn't push the next one back.

Take turns

Pick who's in the rotation, and a chore passes to the next person each time it's done.

Rooms

Group chores by room and see at a glance which parts of the home need attention.

Parents and kids

Parents set the chores and rewards. Kids tick theirs off. A parent approves, and they earn.

Rewards and allowance

Put a value on any chore. The Kids tab shows what's waiting, each kid's last 7 days, and what you owe, with Mark as Paid.

Approve from anywhere

Get a notification when a kid finishes something, and approve it without opening the app.

Jobs up for grabs

Post extras like washing the car. A kid picks one, does it, logs the time with a note, and a parent approves it before it pays.

Missed, not forgotten

A chore not done on its day is missed: it earns nothing and comes back next time instead of piling up. Kids can say they did it; parents decide.

Shared with everyone at home

Any number of parents and kids, on the same list over iCloud.

Who did what

Activity shows who did what this week, this month or all time, and how often chores get done on time.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: tap a chore to finish it without opening the app.

A daily reminder

One notification at the time you choose, with a Done button for each chore that's due.
