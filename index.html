Three parts of lasting health

Progress in one supports the others. MFBP keeps them in one place, on your terms.

Train

Build any routine your way, or start from a library of hundreds of exercises.

  • Animated 3D demos for over 500 exercises
  • Runs, walks and rides with GPS and pace
  • Recovery days that count, because rest is part of training

Fuel

Today's calories and protein from Apple Health, next to how you train.

  • Goals from your body weight
  • Works with any food app that shares with Health
  • No meal plans, no fad diets

Mind

How you feel, how you sleep and the habits you choose, at your own pace.

  • One-tap check-ins
  • Sleep, breathing and mindful minutes
  • Habits with targets you set

Meet Mind New

A calmer way to look after the part of health a workout log misses. All of Mind is free.

Check in

Tap how you're feeling and, if you like, what shaped your day. Over time, see your check-ins next to your training (Pro).

Habits you set

Alcohol-free days, a screens-off wind-down, sleep and mindful minutes, each against a target you choose. Habits count by the week, so a missed day never breaks your week.

Rest is part of the plan

Last night's sleep from Apple Health, or log it yourself if you don't wear a tracker. After a run of training days, Today suggests a rest day, and a rest day you plan keeps your streak going.

Breathe and learn

Guided box breathing, the physiological sigh and 4-7-8, saved to Apple Health as mindful minutes. Short guides on stress, sleep, light and screens, rest and alcohol, each with its sources.

Everything you log in Mind stays on your iPhone and is never synced to iCloud. MFBP offers general wellness guidance and isn't a substitute for professional medical or mental health care. Support & Resources is always one tap away in the app.
