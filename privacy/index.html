Privacy Policy

MFBP · Last updated September 24, 2026

The short version: MFBP doesn't run any servers and doesn't collect your data. There's no account and no MFBP backend. By default everything stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple Health, or share a workout, your data moves only between Apple's services, your own devices, and the people you choose to send it to. It never passes through us.

What we collect

Nothing. MFBP does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server we operate. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no MFBP-run backend of any kind.

Where your data lives

By default, everything you create in MFBP (exercises, workouts, workout history, notes, and your Fuel goals) is stored only on your device, using Apple's local on-device storage. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

MFBP also offers optional features that use Apple services or move data off your device, described below. None of them sends anything to MFBP or to any server we operate.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, MFBP uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your exercises, workouts, history, and progress across your own devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account, encrypted and managed by Apple under your Apple ID. MFBP has no access to it, and it is never sent to us or to any third party. Sync is off by default and takes effect the next time you launch the app after turning it on.

iCloud sync never includes data MFBP reads from Apple Health, and it doesn't include your Fuel goals.

Apple Health (optional)

MFBP works with Apple Health in two separate ways. Each has its own setting, and each only works after you grant permission in Apple's Health permission screen. You can review or revoke that permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

Saving workouts to Health. If you turn on Sync to Apple Health, MFBP writes each completed workout to Health as a workout entry, tagged as strength, cardio, or cross training based on the exercises involved.

Reading nutrition and activity (Fuel). If you turn on Fuel (Nutrition on Today), MFBP reads the following from Health:

  • dietary energy (calories), protein, carbohydrates, total fat, fiber, and water
  • active and resting energy burned
  • body weight

MFBP uses this only to show your daily calories and macros, what your workouts burned, and your trends, and to suggest calorie and protein goals. This data usually comes from other apps and devices you've connected to Health, such as a food-tracking app or an Apple Watch. MFBP has no connection to those apps and never contacts them.

Health data MFBP reads is processed on your device. It is not sent to MFBP, sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising or marketing, or given to the on-device AI features. MFBP doesn't copy it into its own database or into iCloud. The one exception is a small summary of today's numbers, described under Widgets below. MFBP never writes nutrition or energy data to Health.

Widgets

If you add the Fuel widget, MFBP saves a small summary of today's fuel on your device, in a storage area shared only with MFBP's own widget. The summary holds calories and macros against your goals, and when they were last updated. It lets the widget display without reading Health itself. The summary is replaced as the day's numbers change, and it's removed when you turn Fuel off.

A widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen can be seen by anyone who can see your device, including while it's locked. Remove the widget if you'd rather keep these numbers private.

Apple Watch companion

If you use the MFBP Watch app, it mirrors your active workout (exercise, step, and set progress) to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, and relays pause/resume/skip controls back to your phone. If Fuel is on, it also shows today's calories left and protein between workouts. This stays between your own iPhone and your own Watch. It is not sent to MFBP or anyone else.

Sharing workouts

You can send a workout to someone as a file (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or any app in the share sheet), or as a message in Messages using MFBP's iMessage app. A shared workout contains the workout's name and, for each exercise: its name and type, sets, reps, weights, durations, rest times, and any notes you've added to its sets. It doesn't include your workout history, Health data, or anything else about you.

Shared workouts travel only through the Apple service or app you pick, directly to the people you choose. MFBP never sees them. To offer your workouts inside Messages, MFBP keeps a read-only copy of your saveable workouts in a storage area on your device shared only with its own Messages extension.

When you open a workout someone sent you, MFBP shows a preview and saves nothing until you confirm the import. A preview of a workout file can also appear in Files and Quick Look.

Microphone & speech recognition

If you use the "Describe it" feature to dictate an exercise or workout, MFBP uses your device's microphone and Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to transcribe what you say. Apple Intelligence on your device then turns that text into structured fields. All of this happens entirely on your device. Your voice recording and its transcription are never sent to MFBP, to Apple's servers, or to anyone else.

Subscriptions

MFBP Pro is purchased and managed through your Apple ID via the App Store. Apple handles payment, and MFBP never receives your payment details or identity. The app only checks on your device whether your subscription is active.

Analytics & tracking

MFBP does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We don't know how many times you've opened the app, what you've logged, or who you are. Aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics may be visible to us only through Apple's standard App Store Connect reporting. Apple provides these to all developers, and they're never tied to your identity.

Third parties

MFBP does not integrate with or share data with any third-party service. The only exceptions are the optional Apple services described above (iCloud/CloudKit, Apple Health, and Messages), which operate under your own Apple ID and are never routed through MFBP. Fuel can show nutrition that other apps have saved to Apple Health, but MFBP reads it from Health only and has no connection to those apps.

Children's privacy

MFBP does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
