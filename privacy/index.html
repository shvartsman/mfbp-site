Privacy Policy

MFBP · Last updated August 26, 2026

The short version: MFBP doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no server, and nothing about you or your workouts ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. MFBP does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no cloud backend of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in MFBP — exercises, workouts, and workout history — is stored only on your device, using Apple's local on-device storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Microphone & speech recognition

If you use the "Describe it" feature to dictate an exercise or workout, MFBP uses your device's microphone and Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to transcribe what you say, and Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence to turn that text into structured fields. All of this happens entirely on your device. Your voice recording and its transcription are never sent to MFBP, to Apple's servers, or to anyone else.

Analytics & tracking

MFBP does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We don't know how many times you've opened the app, what you've logged, or who you are. Aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics may be visible to us only through Apple's standard App Store Connect reporting, provided to all developers and never tied to your identity.

Third parties

MFBP does not integrate with or share data with any third-party service.

Children's privacy

MFBP does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13 — because it does not collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@mfbp.io.
