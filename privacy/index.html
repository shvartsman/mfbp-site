Privacy Policy

MFBP · Last updated September 2, 2026

The short version: MFBP doesn't run any servers and doesn't collect your data. There's no account and no MFBP backend. By default everything stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync or Apple Health, your data moves only between Apple's services and your own devices — it never passes through us.

What we collect

Nothing. MFBP does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server we operate. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no MFBP-run backend of any kind.

Where your data lives

By default, everything you create in MFBP — exercises, workouts, and workout history — is stored only on your device, using Apple's local on-device storage. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

MFBP also offers two optional, opt-in features that move data off your device, described below. Neither sends anything to MFBP or to any server we operate.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, MFBP uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your exercises, workouts, history, and progress across your own devices, stored in your private iCloud account. This data is encrypted and managed by Apple under your Apple ID — MFBP has no access to it, and it is never sent to us or to any third party. Sync is off by default and takes effect on your next launch of the app after enabling it.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Apple Health sync in Settings, MFBP writes each completed workout to the Health app as a workout entry, tagged as strength, cardio, or cross training based on the exercises involved. This data is stored by Apple's HealthKit on your device and, if you've enabled iCloud for Health, synced through your own iCloud account. MFBP only writes workout data to Health when you turn this on — it is not sent to us.

Apple Watch companion

If you use the MFBP Watch app, it mirrors your active workout — exercise, step, and set progress — to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, and relays pause/resume/skip controls back to your phone. This stays between your own iPhone and your own Watch; it is not sent to MFBP or anyone else.

Microphone & speech recognition

If you use the "Describe it" feature to dictate an exercise or workout, MFBP uses your device's microphone and Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to transcribe what you say, and Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence to turn that text into structured fields. All of this happens entirely on your device. Your voice recording and its transcription are never sent to MFBP, to Apple's servers, or to anyone else.

Analytics & tracking

MFBP does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We don't know how many times you've opened the app, what you've logged, or who you are. Aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics may be visible to us only through Apple's standard App Store Connect reporting, provided to all developers and never tied to your identity.

Third parties

MFBP does not integrate with or share data with any third-party service, other than the optional Apple services described above (iCloud/CloudKit and Apple Health), which operate under your own Apple ID and are never routed through MFBP.

Children's privacy

MFBP does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13 — because it does not collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@mfbp.io.
